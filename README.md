# Student Housing Finder

A one-screen dashboard that helps students anywhere in the US compare places to live near campus.

**[Open the app](https://arushiSH5-cyber.github.io/student-housing-finder/)**

## What it does
- **Filter by your needs**: campus/area, max rent per person, room vs. shared vs. alone vs. on campus, furnished, in-unit laundry, ≤10 min walk, pets, utilities included.
- **Shows your real monthly cost**: rent + your utilities + your food, plus move-in cash (deposit + first month + application fee). You enter these numbers because they differ by city and state.
- **Shows how current each price is**: green = checked in the last 60 days, amber = the listing page has no date, grey = old and may be gone.
- **Lets you add your own places**: add or edit them in the app, or fill the CSV template in a spreadsheet and import it. Export to back up your list or share it with roommates.
- **Gives safety checks**: what to ask before you apply and scam red flags.

Your data is saved only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded.

## Example data
The app opens with a real example: 21 places near SJSU and Santa Clara University (San Jose, CA). Prices were checked on Oct 13, 2026, from SJSU official listings, Apartments.com, ApartmentFinder, RentCafe, an Off-Campus Universe guide and campus housing rates. Use **⋯ → Start empty** to begin your own list.

## Run locally
It's one HTML file with no build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser.
